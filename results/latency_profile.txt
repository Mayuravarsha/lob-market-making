CAVEAT -- clock resolution on this machine: this binary times every single
operation with std::chrono::steady_clock, which on Windows maps to
QueryPerformanceCounter. On this machine QPC's frequency is 10,000,000 Hz,
i.e. its own tick is 100 ns -- coarser than the ~1-2 ns operations being
timed. That is why clock overhead reads 100.0 ns (vs 20.0 ns on the Linux
box the original numbers came from) and why p50/p90 below are quantized to
multiples of 100 ns, including literal 0.0 ns entries -- those are "faster
than one clock tick", not "zero cost". Treat p50/p90 here as lower-resolution
than the ones previously measured on Linux; the tail (p99.9, max) and the
overall shape are still meaningful since they're multiple clock ticks wide.
